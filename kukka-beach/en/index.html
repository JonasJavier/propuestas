The house

An Italian chef with his feet in the Caribbean

Kukka Beach is the Alkquimia group's beach restaurant in Bayahibe. The kitchen is signed by chef Paolo De Vita: pasta the Italian way, whole catch of the day and Caribbean lobster, served at colourful tables under the roof or right on the sand.

The name comes from Kukka, the house parrot. That's why, once you reach the bar, you'll be counting parrots everywhere.

    Before you come

    Questions we hear a lot

    Still have one? Message us and we'll answer on WhatsApp.

    Ask on WhatsApp
    Do I need to book?

    For lunch or a drink, come whenever you like. For dinner on the sand at sunset, or if you're coming as a group, better book: those tables fill up first.

    Do prices include taxes?

    No. Just like on the printed menu, prices are subject to 18% ITBIS (VAT) and the 10% legal service charge. On our menu you can see the total with taxes in one tap, before you order.

    Are fish and lobster priced by the pound?

    Yes. Red snapper, yellowtail snapper and lobster are charged by the pound, depending on the weight of the piece. Ask for the weight before it's cooked and you'll know the total from the start.

    How can I pay?

    Cash in Dominican pesos or US dollars, and card.

    Can I bring children?

    Of course. There are high chairs, and the menu has chicken fingers and nuggets, fries and fruit milkshakes.

    Where do I park?

    In the car park at the entrance to Bayahibe public beach. From there it's a few steps across the sand. If you're staying at one of the beach resorts, you can walk along the shore.

    Do you host birthdays or groups?

    Yes: birthdays, anniversaries, private dinners on the sand and groups. Tell us your idea and we'll send you a proposal.
